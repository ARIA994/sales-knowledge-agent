# Competitor: Recruitee

Last updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Product Marketing

Recruitee is a dedicated ATS, Dutch, strong in the Benelux and in SMB and mid-market across Europe. This is our closest like-for-like competitor.

## Where Recruitee wins

Price at the low end. Cheaper for small teams with a handful of open roles. Simple setup, good career page builder, strong brand in the Netherlands.

## Where Hirelane wins

Volume. Recruitee customers who grow past roughly 40 concurrent open roles start reporting slow bulk operations and reporting gaps. This is our most common source of switching customers.

Approval workflows. Recruitee's approval chains are limited; ours support up to 5 levels with conditions on department and salary band.

Reporting. Ours goes deeper on stage conversion and source effectiveness per role.

## How to position

This is a displacement sale, so the trigger matters. Ask what specifically broke: usually bulk actions, reporting, or approvals. Anchor on that one thing rather than a feature comparison.

Migration from Recruitee is supported: candidates, jobs and stage history import via CSV. Historical interview notes do not migrate.
