# Hirelane Pricing and Packaging

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Owner: Revenue Operations

## Plans

Hirelane is sold per employee per month, billed annually. All plans include unlimited job postings.

| Plan | Price (per employee / month) | Minimum | Included seats for recruiters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | EUR 4.50 | 200 employees | 3 |
| Growth | EUR 7.00 | 200 employees | 10 |
| Enterprise | Custom, from EUR 9.50 | 500 employees | Unlimited |

A company with 600 employees on Growth pays 600 x EUR 7.00 x 12 = EUR 50,400 per year.

## What separates the plans

Core: job postings, candidate pipeline, basic scorecards, email templates, one career page.
Growth: everything in Core plus approval workflows, structured interview scorecards, reporting, API access, SSO, multiple career pages, and up to 5 integrations.
Enterprise: everything in Growth plus custom fields without limit, sandbox environment, dedicated CSM, custom SLA, audit log export, and unlimited integrations.

## Discount policy

Reps may offer up to 10 percent without approval. Discounts between 10 and 20 percent need the Head of Sales. Anything above 20 percent needs the CEO and is only approved for multi-year commitments or reference customers.

Two-year commitment: additional 5 percent.
Three-year commitment: additional 8 percent.

## Billing

Annual billing is standard. Quarterly billing is available on Enterprise at a 7 percent uplift. Monthly billing is not offered on any plan.

Payment terms are 30 days net. Public sector customers may request 60 days; this needs Finance approval.
