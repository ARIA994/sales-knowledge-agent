# Product Overview

Last updated: 2026-07-30
Owner: Product Marketing

Hirelane is an applicant tracking system for companies that hire in volume. It is built for talent teams that fill the same roles repeatedly: warehouse staff, drivers, retail associates, shift workers, and support roles.

## Core capabilities

Candidate pipeline: drag-and-drop stages, bulk actions on up to 500 candidates at once, automatic duplicate detection across applications.

Job distribution: one posting publishes to the career page plus connected job boards. Boards are configured per job, not per account.

Structured interviews: scorecards per role, with required fields before a hiring manager can advance a candidate. Interview kits can be cloned across similar roles.

Mobile: candidates can apply from a phone in under two minutes without creating an account. Hiring managers can review and advance candidates from the mobile app. Recruiters need the desktop app for bulk actions.

Approval workflows: requisition approval chains with up to 5 levels, conditional on department and salary band. Growth and Enterprise only.

Reporting: time to hire, source effectiveness, pipeline conversion per stage, recruiter activity. Reports export to CSV. Scheduled email delivery is available on Enterprise.

## What Hirelane does not do

Hirelane is not an HRIS. It does not handle payroll, contracts, absence management or performance reviews. Customers typically run Hirelane alongside Personio, BambooHR or an HR module in their ERP.

Hirelane does not do background checks or reference checks natively. These run through integration partners.
