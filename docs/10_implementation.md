# Implementation Timeline

Last updated: 2026-05-20
Owner: Customer Success

## Standard timeline

Core: 2 weeks. Self-serve setup with onboarding sessions.
Growth: 4 to 6 weeks.
Enterprise: 8 to 12 weeks depending on integrations and approval complexity.

## What happens in a Growth implementation

Week 1: kickoff, account setup, user provisioning, SSO configuration.
Week 2: job board connections and career page setup.
Weeks 3 and 4: pipeline stages, scorecards and approval workflows configured to the customer's process.
Week 5: data migration if switching from another ATS, plus hiring manager training.
Week 6: go live with support attending the first week of use.

## Data migration

Supported sources: Recruitee, Personio recruiting, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and CSV.

What migrates: candidates, jobs, stage history, application dates, attachments.
What does not migrate: interview notes, email threads, scorecards from the source system, and audit logs.

Migration is included on Growth and Enterprise. On Core it is a paid service.

## What delays implementations

The most common cause is not the software. It is waiting for the customer to decide their own hiring process: who approves what, which stages they want, who sees salary information. Customers who arrive with these decisions made go live in half the time.

Second most common: SSO configuration waiting on the customer's IT team.
