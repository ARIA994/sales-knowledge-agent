# Career Page and Employer Branding

Last updated: 2025-11-03
Owner: Product Marketing

The career page is included on every plan.

## What you can configure

Logo, colours, fonts from a set of 12, hero image, and a free-text section per page. Custom CSS is available on Growth and Enterprise.

Core includes one career page. Growth includes up to 5, typically used for separate brands or countries. Enterprise is unlimited.

Pages are hosted on a hirelane subdomain by default. A custom domain is available on all plans and requires a CNAME record.

## Languages

A career page can be published in multiple languages. Job descriptions are entered per language; there is no automatic translation.

Supported interface languages: German, English, Dutch, French.

## Application form

The default application form asks for name, email, phone and CV. Additional fields are configurable per job. Knockout questions are supported and can auto-reject candidates who do not meet a requirement.

Candidates can apply with a CV file, a LinkedIn profile import, or by filling the form manually. Apply without an account is the default; account creation is optional and off by default.

## Analytics

Page views, application starts, application completions and drop-off per field. Source tracking via UTM parameters. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel can be connected on Growth and Enterprise.
