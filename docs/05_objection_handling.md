# Objection Handling

Last updated: 2026-09-10
Owner: Sales Enablement

## "It is too expensive"

Do not lead with a discount. Ask what they are comparing against. In most cases the comparison is either doing nothing, or an HRIS recruiting module they already pay for.

Reframe to cost per hire. A company hiring 60 people per year with an average time to hire of 42 days is losing productive days, not licence fees. If they can quantify the cost of a vacant shift role per day, the maths usually closes itself.

If the budget genuinely is not there, offer Core with an upgrade path rather than discounting Growth.

## "We already use Personio"

Personio is an HRIS with a recruiting module, not a dedicated ATS. The module works for low-volume hiring. It struggles when a company runs more than roughly 20 open roles at once, or needs bulk actions, or hires the same role repeatedly.

We integrate with Personio, so this is rarely either-or. The usual outcome is Personio for HR records and Hirelane for hiring. Do not position it as a replacement for Personio.

## "Now is not the right time"

Find out whether this is budget timing or hiring timing. If they have a seasonal peak, the implementation timeline matters: 4 to 6 weeks for Growth means starting 2 months before the peak, not during it.

If it is genuinely the wrong quarter, agree a specific date to revisit and put a task in the CRM. Do not leave it open.

## "We need a feature you do not have"

Check the product overview and integrations list before answering. Never promise a feature that is not shipped. If it is on the roadmap, say it is on the roadmap without giving a date.

The most common genuine gaps: SAP SuccessFactors integration, Dutch payroll integration, native background checks.

## "Our hiring managers will not use it"

This is the strongest objection to take seriously, because it is usually true of their current tool. Lead with the mobile approval flow and the fact that hiring managers do not need training to review and advance candidates. Offer a pilot with one department.
