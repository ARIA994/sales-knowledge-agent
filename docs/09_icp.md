# Ideal Customer Profile

Last updated: 2026-09-05
Owner: Revenue Operations

## Firmographics

Company size: 200 to 2,000 employees. Below 200 the value is hard to prove and the deal size does not support the sales cycle. Above 2,000 we usually lose to enterprise suites.

Region: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Netherlands.

Industries: logistics, e-commerce, manufacturing, retail, professional services, technology.

## Buying signals

Strong: more than 50 planned hires in the next 12 months, no dedicated ATS, a stated deadline such as a seasonal peak or a new site opening, recent funding, an outdated ATS that hiring managers refuse to use.

Weak: general interest, no hiring volume stated, a junior contact with no buying authority.

## Buyer titles

Decision maker: Head of Talent, Talent Acquisition Lead, HR Director, Head of People, CHRO, VP People.
Influencer: IT Director, hiring managers, Finance for budget sign-off.
Not a buyer: recruiters, HR generalists, office managers, and anyone asking about open roles at Hirelane itself.

## Disqualifiers

Staffing and recruiting agencies. They want to resell or use the tool for client hiring, which our terms do not permit.

Companies under 50 employees.

Job seekers using the demo form to ask about roles at Hirelane.

Regions outside DACH and the Netherlands, unless the company has a DACH entity that would be the contracting party.
