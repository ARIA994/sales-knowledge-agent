# Competitor: Personio

Last updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Product Marketing

Personio is an HR operating system for SMBs in Europe, strongest in the DACH region. Recruiting is one module among payroll, absence, contracts and performance.

## Where Personio wins

One system for all HR data. Strong brand recognition in Germany and Austria. Already installed at many of our prospects, which makes the buying decision harder, not easier.

Pricing is bundled, so recruiting appears to cost nothing extra once they own the platform.

## Where Hirelane wins

Volume hiring. Personio's recruiting module is built for a few open roles at a time. Bulk actions, high-volume pipelines and repeated hiring for the same role are where customers hit its limits.

Structured interviews and scorecards. Personio has basic evaluation forms; we have role-specific scorecards with required fields and interview kits.

Hiring manager adoption. Personio's recruiting UI is designed for HR users. Our mobile review flow is designed for managers who open it twice a week.

Reporting depth on hiring specifically: stage conversion, source effectiveness per role, recruiter activity.

## How to position

Not a replacement. The winning message is that Personio stays as the system of record and Hirelane handles hiring. We integrate, so data flows both ways and hires land in Personio automatically.

Avoid attacking Personio directly. The buyer chose it, and criticising that choice puts them on the defensive.
