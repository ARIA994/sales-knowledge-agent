# Competitor: Workday

Last updated: 2026-08-22
Owner: Product Marketing

Workday is an enterprise HCM suite. We meet it at the top end of our range, usually at companies with 1,000 or more employees.

## Where Workday wins

Breadth. If a company has already standardised on Workday for finance and HR, replacing any part of it is a political decision, not a product decision.

Global compliance and multi-country payroll, which we do not touch at all.

## Where Hirelane wins

Speed of implementation. Our Growth implementation is 4 to 6 weeks. A Workday recruiting rollout is typically measured in months.

Cost for volume roles. Workday's per-employee pricing at enterprise scale makes it expensive to give access to every hiring manager in a warehouse or retail network.

Usability for non-HR users. This is the most common complaint we hear from Workday customers: hiring managers avoid the tool, so recruiters do the work manually anyway.

## How to position

Rarely a rip-and-replace. The realistic deal is Hirelane for high-volume operational hiring while Workday remains for corporate roles and as the system of record. We support hire export to Workday in one direction.

Be careful: this is a two-system story, and some buyers will reject it on principle. Qualify early whether they will accept two tools.
