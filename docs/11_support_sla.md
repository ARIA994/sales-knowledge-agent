# Support and SLA

Last updated: 2026-04-11
Owner: Customer Success

## Support hours

Core and Growth: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 CET.
Enterprise: 24 hours on weekdays, plus weekend cover for severity 1 issues.

Support is in German and English. Dutch support is handled in English.

## Response targets

| Severity | Definition | Core and Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service unavailable | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| 2 | Major function broken, no workaround | 8 hours | 4 hours |
| 3 | Function broken with a workaround | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| 4 | Question or request | 3 business days | 2 business days |

These are response targets, not resolution targets.

## Uptime

The contractual uptime commitment is 99.5 percent monthly on Core and Growth, and 99.9 percent on Enterprise. Planned maintenance is excluded and is announced 5 days in advance, scheduled outside business hours in CET.

Service credits apply on Enterprise only: 5 percent of monthly fees per 0.1 percent below the commitment, capped at 25 percent.

## Escalation

Enterprise customers have a named CSM and a direct escalation path. Growth customers escalate through support, which routes to the CSM team.
