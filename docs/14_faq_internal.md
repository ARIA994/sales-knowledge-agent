# Internal Sales FAQ

Last updated: 2026-09-12
Owner: Sales Enablement

**Can a customer trial the product?**
Yes. 14-day trial with sample data, no credit card. A trial with the customer's own data requires a signed order form with a 30-day opt-out.

**Do we offer a free tier?**
No. There is no free plan and no freemium version.

**Can we sell to a company below the 200 employee minimum?**
The minimum is contractual, not technical. Below 200 employees, a deal needs Head of Sales approval and is usually only worth it if the company is growing fast or is a reference name.

**What happens at renewal if the customer has grown?**
Seats are trued up at renewal based on the employee count at that date. We do not bill mid-term for growth.

**Can a customer downgrade?**
At renewal only. Mid-term downgrades are not supported.

**Is there a setup fee?**
No setup fee on Core and Growth. Enterprise implementations with custom integrations may carry a one-time professional services fee, quoted case by case.

**How long is the standard contract?**
12 months, auto-renewing with 30 days notice before the renewal date.

**Who signs the DPA?**
It is part of the standard contract. No separate signature needed unless the customer insists on their own template, which goes to Legal.
